Canvas controls

Select an input method to see how to inspect and correct particles.

  • Left click / V:Select particles

    Left-click a circle. Shift-click to add or remove circles from the selection.

  • Left-drag:Move particles

    Left-drag a selected circle to move the entire selection.

  • Middle-drag / H:Pan the image

    Middle-drag anywhere, or choose the Hand tool and left-drag.

  • Right-drag / C:Add a particle

    Right-drag across a particle’s diameter, or use the Draw Circle tool and left-drag.

  • Wheel:Zoom

    Scroll the wheel to zoom toward or away from the pointer.

  • R:Set the scale

    Choose the Scale tool, then left-drag along the image’s scale bar.

Shortcuts: V Select · H Hand · C Draw · R Scale · F Fit view · Delete Remove selected

  • Tap:Select a particle

    Tap a circle to select it. Tap empty image space to clear the selection.

  • Drag particle:Move a particle

    Drag a selected circle to reposition it.

  • Drag canvas / Hand:Pan the image

    Drag empty image space, or choose the Hand tool and drag anywhere.

  • Draw Circle tool:Add a particle

    Choose the Draw Circle tool, then drag from one edge of the particle to the other.

  • Two fingers:Zoom and pan

    Pinch with two fingers to zoom; move both fingers together to pan.

  • Scale tool:Set the scale

    Choose the Scale tool, then drag along the image’s scale bar.
